Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Sputter Bets (sputterbets.com, including the college page) shows NFL and college football predictions. You can read everything without an account. This page explains what we collect if you sign in, what becomes public, who processes it for us, and how to get it deleted. Sputter Bets is run by Divine Davis, who is the data controller.

The short version: if you sign in with Google we store your Google account's email address and name, a display name made from that name, the teams and players you star, and your picks. Your display name, your picks and your win–loss record are public by default — on the leaderboard, in “Friends' picks”, in “how did everyone else call this?”, and on your shareable picks card. You can rename yourself or hide from all of those in “My picks”. We don't sell data, run ads, or use analytics or tracking cookies.

What is public

While your profile is set to public (the default), anyone — signed in or not — can see your display name, your picks for each week, and your win–loss record: on the leaderboard, in the players list and “Friends' picks”, in the per-game “others' picks” line, and on your shareable card at sputterbets.com/s/<share ID>, whose link preview also shows your display name and record. Your email address, Google ID and photo are never shown to anyone. Turn on “Hidden from the leaderboard” in My picks to remove yourself from every public view; your share link then stops showing your picks.

Who processes your data

  • Google (Google LLC) — the sign-in provider. Google's own privacy policy covers what it records when you sign in.
  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc.) — hosts our database and handles sign-in, in the United States.
  • DigitalOcean (DigitalOcean, LLC) — hosts the website server in the United States.

No other third party receives your data. There are no ads, analytics, trackers or third-party scripts on the site. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

How long we keep it

Account data, stars and picks are kept until you ask us to delete your account. Web server logs are rotated and deleted after about two weeks; Supabase keeps its own sign-in and API logs for a short period under its policies.

Deleting your data

You can unstar anything and remove an unlocked pick at any time on the site. To delete your whole account — profile, picks, stars and the sign-in record — email divinejdavis@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Your rights

Everyone: you can ask what we hold about you, get a copy, correct it, or have it deleted, by emailing the address below. We answer within 30 days and never charge or treat you differently for asking.

California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know, access, correct and delete personal information, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share it, and we collect no sensitive personal information. You may use an authorised agent.

EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): our legal bases are performance of a contract (running your account and pick'em when you sign in) and legitimate interests (security logs). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO). Data is processed in the United States; our processors use standard contractual clauses for transfers.

Children

Sputter Bets is about sports betting lines and is meant for adults. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, email us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted on the site.
